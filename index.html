How to explore

Three choices get you to any map in the atlas.

1

Pick an index

Choose one of the ten ocean or atmospheric modes from the grid above or the navigation bar.

2

Pick a season and period

DJF, MAM, JJA, or SON — over the long-term, intermediate-term, or short-term analysis window.

3

Pick a product

Teleconnection maps show the average linear relationship via regression. Agreement composites show where an index's extreme years coincide with hydroclimate extremes.

New to the atlas? Start with the worked example comparing ENSO and AMO over North America.

Coverage at a glance

Which indices and hydroclimate products are available over each analysis period. Note that SST-based and HGT/SLP-based indices do not share a baseline outside the short-term window.

Period Years Indices available Hydroclimate fields
Long-term (LT) 1901–2024 ENSO, PDO, AMO, IOD, TPI (SST-based) CRU: precipitation, temperature, PDSI
Intermediate-term (IT) 1941–2024 AO, AAO, NAO, NPI, SAM (HGT/SLP-based) CRU: precipitation, temperature, PDSI
Short-term (ST) 1981–2024 All ten indices CRU + GLEAM soil moisture + GPCP precipitation

All products are provided for four seasons: DJF, MAM, JJA, and SON.

Data access

All HYDRA products are freely available. Gridded fields are provided in NetCDF-4; index time series and extreme states are additionally provided in CSV.
