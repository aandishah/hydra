ENSO at a glance

Ocean-based index
Full name
El Niño–Southern Oscillation
Definition
Area-averaged SST anomalies over the Niño 3.4 region (5°N–5°S, 170°–120°W).
Data source
HadISST 1.1 sea surface temperature
Analysis periods
Long term 1901–2024 · Short term 1981–2024
Key reference
Trenberth et al. (1997)
